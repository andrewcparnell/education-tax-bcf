Quick Guide

This explorer summarises causal estimates across 121 countries (1985-2024) of how changes in public per-student university spending affect national tax revenue.

Simple vs Advanced Mode

  • By default, the app shows the headline 40-year career return per entering student cohort with 80% and 95% credible intervals.
  • Switch Advanced on in the top-right bar at any time to unlock 5-year and 10-year annual horizons, discounted and conservative accounting rules, all four funding definitions (Z1 to Z4), tax-share outcomes (Y3 and Y4), and custom national baseline inputs.

Total Tax vs Income Tax Only

  • Total Tax: Captures all non-resource Exchequer receipts (income, payroll/social insurance, corporate, and VAT).
  • Income Tax Only: Conservative floor counting direct personal income and corporate profit taxes alone.
  • Note that 10-year aggregate tax-to-GDP changes carry substantial macroeconomic uncertainty, shown directly by the error bars on every chart.
