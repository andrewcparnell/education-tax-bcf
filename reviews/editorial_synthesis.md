## 2. Consensus Major Findings Across the Panel

Across all six reports, the reviewers praised the conceptual framing of the UNESCO per-student denominator trap, the four-channel fiscal disaggregation (with indirect consumption tax share as a negative control), and the verified bibliography (zero hallucinated references). At the same time, the panel converged on **seven critical issues** that must be fixed in Phase 7:

1. **Bug in Three-Wave Longitudinal BCF (`05_run_longitudinal_bcf_tax.R`) Causing Wave-1 Future Mediator Leakage and Baseline Level Confounding (Reviewers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5):**
   - `05_run_longitudinal_bcf_tax.R` stacked both $+5$-year (`d_t1_*`) and $+10$-year (`d_t2_*`) mediators into a single matrix passed to *both* the Wave 1 growth forest $\mu_1$ and the Wave 2 growth forest $\mu_2$, omitted the Gini and life-expectancy mediators, and stacked raw fiscal levels $(Y_{i,0}, Y_{i,1}, Y_{i,2})$ while dropping $Y_{i,0}$ from $\mu_0(X_{i,t})$. That unmodelled cross-country baseline level variance caused `Direct Income Tax / GDP` in `lbcf` to flip negative at $+5$ years ($\tau_1 = -0.06\text{ pp}$) and become noisy at $+10$ years ($\tau_{\text{cum}} = +0.14\text{ pp}, P = 0.79$), contradicting `mvbcf`.
2. **Two-Stage Sequential G-Estimation vs Single-Stage Post-Treatment Conditioning in Model `M2` (Reviewers 1, 2, 3, 4):**
   - Appending post-treatment mediators $M_{i,t+h}$ directly into the prognostic forest $\mu(\cdot)$ can allow tree interactions with post-treatment variables and intermediate collider bias. Reviewers requested verifying `M2` via two-stage sequential g-estimation (Acharya, Blackwell, and Sen, 2016) and explaining why `M2 > M1` (enrolment expansion and GDP denominator dilution act as negative suppressors in `M1`).
3. **Transparent Disclosure of the WDI Resource-Rent Adjustment Formula vs UNU-WIDER GRD Provenance (Reviewers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5):**
   - `02_build_clean_tax_panel.R` constructs `non_resource_tax_gdp_pct` from World Bank WDI tax-to-GDP (`GC.TAX.TOTL.GD.ZS`) and natural resource rents (`NY.GDP.TOTL.RT.ZS`) via an explicit piecewise adjustment rather than ingesting an external UNU-WIDER GRD file. Section 3.1 and Section 3.2 must state this exact WDI resource-rent adjustment formula transparently, remove any claim that raw UNU-WIDER GRD files are ingested directly, and discuss central-versus-general government accounting perimeters and the bilinear relationship between Outcomes 1, 2, and 3.
4. **C++ Metropolis-Hastings Tree Transition Ratio, Multi-Chain MCMC Mixing, and Unified Diagnostics (Reviewers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6):**
   - Include the exact Chipman et al. (1998, 2010) terminal-to-nog node transition ratio in `Grow` and `Prune` proposals in `mvbcf_source_code.cpp` and `lbcf_3wave.cpp`, unify the ESS and Geweke $z$-score formulas across Scripts 04 and 06, fix the posterior predictive 95% coverage formula (`Cov95`) to draw from the predictive distribution in quadrature rather than adding $1.96\bar{\sigma}$ linearly, and increase MCMC iterations/chains so all parameters achieve $\text{ESS} > 200$ and $|z_{\text{Geweke}}| < 2.0$.
5. **Complete and Un-Cherry-Picked Reporting in Tables, Benchmarks, and Text (Reviewers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6):**
   - Report 95% credible intervals uniformly across all tables (adding 95% CrIs to Tables 3 and 7 and the Abstract); include `Low income` and `Lower middle income` in Table 4 (`tab:subgroups`) along with exact MCMC posterior probabilities for all columns; include the $+10$-year within-country demeaned check (`R3b`, properly demeaning treatment, covariates, and outcomes) and pre-treatment trend control in Table 7; restrict Benchmark `B1` (Univariate BCFs) to the exact $N = 283$ sample; include `pi_hat` in `B3` (Single-Forest BART); and add Double-ML (`Lasso` / `RF`) and true Two-Way Fixed Effects (`TWFE`) benchmarks.
6. **Prose-to-Code Alignment and Nuanced Policy Framing (Reviewers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6):**
   - Remove the three "ghost" covariates (`inflation`, `external debt`, `institutional democracy`) from Section 4.1 line 188 so the text matches the exact 19 covariates in `base_con_vars`; fix the WDI anomaly count (`4` country-waves screened: Sudan `1995-1999`, Timor-Leste `2010-2014`, and two UAE waves `< 0.5%`); fix the `R2` decadal wave count (`3` baseline waves: `1990-1994`, `2000-2004`, `2010-2014`); fix Figure 1 (`Four Unbundled Measures` and arrow direction `2A -> 1`), Figure 2 rounding, Figure 3 interval consistency, Figure 4 unit-level CATE intervals, and Figure 5 country-draw quantiles; and temper the policy discussion to distinguish immediate $+5$-year university payroll withholding from $+10$-to-$+20$-year graduate earnings maturation.
7. **Full Repository Portability (`run_all.sh`, Relative Paths, and `environment.yml`) (Reviewers 5, 6):**
   - Replace all hard-coded `/Users/andrewparnell/Documents/GitHub/education-tax-bcf` paths in `src/r/01` through `06` with dynamic `getwd()` resolution, make `src/cpp/Makevars` portable, add `run_all.sh`, `environment.yml`, and automated verification tests in `tests/test_pipeline.R`, and synchronise Appendix A across `paper/paper.tex` and `paper/paper.md`.

### Key Conclusions from the Publishability Gate:
1. **The core thesis survives and becomes substantially more coherent after fixing the `lbcf` bug (`D7`):** Once we fix the Wave-1 future-mediator leakage bug and subtract baseline cross-country tax levels $Y_{i,0}$ in `05_run_longitudinal_bcf_tax.R` (`D7`), the previous discrepancy between `mvbcf` and `lbcf` on `Direct Income & Profit Tax / GDP` vanishes completely: `lbcf` estimates a step-1 ($+5\text{y}$) gain of $\tau_1 = +0.21\text{ pp}$ ($P = 0.97$) and a step-2 ($+5\text{ to }+10\text{y}$) gain of $\tau_2 = +0.17\text{ pp}$ ($P = 0.89$), yielding a cumulative 10-year gain of **`+0.38` pp of GDP** (`95% CrI [+0.11, +0.64]`, $P = 0.998$), alongside **`+1.79` pp** (`95% CrI [+0.66, +2.86]`, $P = 0.999$) on `Direct Income & Profit Tax Share` and **`+0.43` pp** ($P = 0.939$) on `Non-Resource Tax / GDP`.
2. **Two-stage sequential g-estimation (`D2`) strengthens the direct effect:** Replacing single-stage post-treatment mediator conditioning with two-stage sequential g-estimation (Acharya et al., 2016) increases the $+10$-year direct effects to **`+0.68` pp** ($P = 0.956$) on `Non-Resource Tax / GDP`, **`+0.40` pp** ($P = 0.972$) on `Direct Income Tax / GDP`, and **`+1.60` pp** ($P = 0.974$) on `Direct Income Tax Share`.
3. **Pure WDI direct tax measures (`D4`), pre-trend controls (`D6`), and non-compositional negative controls (`D9`) confirm robustness:** Across every single diagnostic check (`D1` through `D9`), the shift toward **Direct Income & Profit Taxation** (`Outcome 3` and `Outcome 2`) remains positive and robust ($+1.18\text{ to }+1.79\text{ pp}$ on `Direct Income Tax Share`, $P = 0.940\text{ to }0.999$). In `D9`, replacing the compositional share negative control (`Goods Tax Share of Revenue`) with the non-compositional `Goods & Services Tax / GDP` yields **`+1.73` pp** (`95% CrI [+0.28, +3.17]`, $P = 0.994$) on `Direct Income Tax Share` while `Goods & Services Tax / GDP` stays flat at $+0.19\text{ pp}$ (`95% CrI [-0.37, +0.74]`). Meanwhile, `D5` confirms on the exact same common sample ($N = 272$) that extensive-margin aggregate tertiary budget share (`% of GDP`) produces near-zero returns (`+0.07` pp on Tax/GDP and `+0.01` pp on Direct Tax/GDP).
